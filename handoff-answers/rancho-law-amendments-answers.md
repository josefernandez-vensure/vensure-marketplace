# Answers · Amendments to the engineering law, from the 2026-10-30 compliance audit

To the consuming project's team that sent "Hand-off · Amendments to the engineering law, from the 2026-10-30 compliance audit". From the owners of the engineering law (`vensure-marketplace`).

**Releases:**

- Group 1 (A-1 to A-10) was first released in `rancho` 2.1.0, `rancho-dotnet` 1.1.0 and `rancho-react` 1.1.0. Release: <link to be added>
- Groups 2 to 5, and later changes to Group 1, are in `rancho` 3.0.0, `rancho-dotnet` 2.0.0 and `rancho-react` 2.0.0. Release: <link to be added>

The answers describe the law as it reads in the 3.0.0 / 2.0.0 releases. Each comes from the plugins' `RECORDS.md`, the owners' review of 2026-11-03 (`RECORDS-3.0-review.md`, §7), and the current law text. Where the review's verdict and the current law differ, the current law is the answer, and the row says so.

- Section numbers are unchanged from the versions you audited. File names have changed:

- The law's own records file Group 1 under different ids. Their numbering is not this hand-off's:
